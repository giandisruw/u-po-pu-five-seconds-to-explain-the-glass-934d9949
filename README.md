A house cat's five-second defense, moments after sweeping a glass off the table. No apology, no confession — just the most convincing defense a cat can make.

## The entry

> That glass had been mocking me all morning with its transparency, and I finally called its bluff.

97 characters. One sentence. Pure cat.

## Run it

```bash
python3 main.py
```

It prints exactly the entry sentence to stdout, nothing else.

## How it was chosen

20 candidate one-liners were written, then played through a 3-round Swiss tournament judged by the Caffeine Inference router (blind paired comparisons), with a best-of-3 final between the two 3–0 finishers. The winner went 3–0 in the Swiss and 2–1 in the final. Full per-game scores are in `tournament.md`; every judge call is logged in `judge_log.jsonl`.
